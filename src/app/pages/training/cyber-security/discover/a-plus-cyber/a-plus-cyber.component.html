a+ Cyber

Start your cybersecurity journey with the CompTIA a+ Cyber course, a beginner-friendly, fast-track program designed to help you build the foundational knowledge you need to confidently begin training for the industry-recognized CompTIA Security+ certification. With no prior experience required, this course is the perfect first step for you to take toward a rewarding career in cybersecurity.

Overview

Building your foundational knowledge

  • Cybersecurity support roles: Understand the responsibilities of cybersecurity teams and how they collaborate to address security threats.
  • Managing systems: Build your skills in configuring, monitoring, and securing Linux and Windows systems to ensure operational efficiency and security.
  • Secure network clients: Learn to configure IP addresses, manage SOHO routers, and secure web browsing.
  • Enterprise campus networks: Gain insights into structured cabling, switches, firewalls, virtualization, and other enterprise network fundamentals.
  • Cybersecurity controls: Explore tools and methods for implementing intrusion detection, incident response, and vulnerability management.
  • Automation scripting: Develop and use Bash scripts to automate tasks and streamline processes.
  • Protecting data: Strengthen your understanding of data security, including backups, database management, and safeguarding critical information.
  • Analyzing and responding to threats: Use tools and techniques to identify vulnerabilities, mitigate risks, and respond to security incidents.
  • Building a cybersecurity foundation: Gain the skills and knowledge to confidently transition into Security+ training. This beginner-friendly course provides a streamlined path to certification training and sets you on the path to a rewarding career in cybersecurity.

Course Details

Duration

31 hours of instruction

Content

24 instructional videos, 29 live labs, 11 assessments, and 110 practice questions

Skill-building resources

Hands-on virtual machine labs, instant feedback on assessments, and progress tracking through performance reports

Recommended experience

No prior experience required — designed for you if you’re just starting out

CompCert assessment

Prove your mastery of key concepts and earn a downloadable Competency Certificate at the end of the course to showcase your achievement

Outcome

Confidence and knowledge to begin training for the CompTIA Security+ certification
